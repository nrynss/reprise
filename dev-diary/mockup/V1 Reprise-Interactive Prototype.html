DATA · IN PLAIN WORDS

What Reprise keeps, and for how long

Private by default

Every episode starts private. Stems, transcripts and threads never leave your account. Publishing is a separate, deliberate act per episode — and publishing shares only the finished render and its cover. The transcript stays yours.

What an erase removes

Erasing an episode deletes every row, both stems, the render and the cover from this studio — and it deletes the provider's copy of the session and every batch transcript we asked for.

One honest caveat: the speech provider soft-deletes on its side. It stops being reachable, and we re-verify deletion on a schedule, but the provider's own retention policy governs their backups.

Guest recordings

A visitor who records episode five gets a real account with one code path — not a lesser shadow of one. Their data expires on a timer the studio sets, and expiry deletes rather than hides.

Sessions end explicitly

Streaming bills on connected time, so the session ends the moment you tap End — and a token cap stops anything the browser abandons. Nothing runs open.
