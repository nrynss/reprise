## What it built

The controller posts the stored stem pair at take end before the
processing handoff. It builds the completion driver from the landed
record route module, posts episode plus both upload ids with rates
after both uploads finish, and navigates only on success. A refusal
keeps the pending pair, shows loud text, and retries through
`retryCompletion` without uploading twice. Harness gains retry,
state, and text.

Proof is `controller-complete.spec.ts` on port 4175. Both cases
pass: end posts one pair and reaches processing, failed first post
refuses loudly then reaches processing after retry. Mutation on the
episode id fails case one, restore returns green.
