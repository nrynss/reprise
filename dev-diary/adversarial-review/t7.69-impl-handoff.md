## What was built

A live load with no `?fixture=1` never falls back to the fixture. A refused or failed episode read parks through `failLoad`: snap stays unready with empty words and title, the notice reads "This episode did not load" with the status, and the error carries the detail. The edit page shows a Retry button whenever the draft is unready with a load error, and Retry reruns `loadFromBackend`. On a 429 the controller reads `Retry-After` and retries once on its own after that many seconds capped at 60 s, then parks if the retry also fails. `?fixture=1` still loads the scripted draft exactly as before. The old fallback test is replaced by three pins: a 500 leaves the draft unready with no fixture words or title and the failure named, a manual retry after a 500 loads the live draft, and a 429 with `Retry-After: 1` followed by 200 loads the live draft. The edit spec pins a 500 detail showing Retry with no scripted title, then loads the live title after Retry. `Retry-After` is read by iterating header entries rather than calling `headers.get`, so the leakage scan stays clean.
