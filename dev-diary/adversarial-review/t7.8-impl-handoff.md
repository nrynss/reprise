## What it built

The drain reclaims expired lease holds each second and the redeploy
gate rolls back in about a minute.

Measured waiter: the in-memory quota slot in the lease manager,
counted by `Active()`. A scratch probe with a real manager at a 3
second cap showed an abandoned lease holding `Active()=1` past its
cap under pure polling, while one reclaim freed it. The probe file
was deleted before commit. The drain log names the waiter, reports
each reclaim, and lets go at budget end. Past-cap holds exit in
seconds. Live sessions keep their full cap. Stop timeout stays 1830.

`redeploy.sh` already bounded its gate at 60 seconds, so the half
hour came from stopping the old container with the full timeout
against a binary that never expired leaked holds. Rollback now stops
the failed container fast (30 seconds), restores the recorded image,
health-checks the restore in the same bound, and always exits 1.
`deploy/redeploy_test.sh` pins all three paths with stubbed tooling.
