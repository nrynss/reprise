## What the commit builds

`SessionCap` runs the browser side stop for one minted session. The page
builds it with the end control path as `finish` and the broker
`max_session_duration_seconds`. At the cap it calls `finish` exactly once
through a `capped` flag. Every tick compares the injected clock reading
against the deadline instead of counting ticks. `wake` re-reads the clock
at once. `stop` cancels the timer. `onWarn` fires once with whole seconds
left, and `warnText` exposes the sentence for the live region.

## Hunted candidates that closed without a pin

- **Timer end against person end, two closes.** Closed. The timer calls
  `finish` once behind its own `capped` flag, and every close path funnels
  into the socket `end` latch (`socket.ts:112`). A person end plus a timer
  fire sends one `session.end`. A timer fire after a person end resolves at
  once on `endedOk` and sends nothing.
- **Clock jumps backward, timer misfires or never fires.** Closed. `tick`
  compares `now` against the deadline and re-arms for the remaining span.
  A backward jump waits out real time, which is the safe direction. No
  branch assumes monotonic progress beyond the comparison.
- **A take end that forgets `stop`, leaking a timer into a later take.**
  Closed for this commit: nothing in this diff wires the page yet, so no
  timer exists to leak. The missing wiring itself is OUT-1 below.
- **`warnText` after the cap reads "ends in 0 seconds".** Closed, not a
  defect. Zero seconds are left, and the page hides the notice on `ended`.
- **`finish` throwing inside a timer callback.** Closed without a finding.
  The passed path is the socket `end`, which latches and returns a promise
  rather than throwing. A speculative throw with no pin is not actionable.
- **`start` after `stop` silently does nothing.** Closed. The contract
