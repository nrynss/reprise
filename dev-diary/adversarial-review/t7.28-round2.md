## The remediator's stated gaps

**Rendered words are not tied to a specific render.** An acceptable limit for this task, not a
finding. Analysis replaces the rendered words whole on each run (`internal/analysis/store.go:43`).
At this hash nothing starts analysis at all, because `analysisFunc` has no caller
(`cmd/reprise/main.go:1160`). A second render needs a draft or a failed episode
(`internal/episode/lifecycle.go:60-72`). Stale words therefore need render, analysis, failure,
changed decisions, a new render, and a failed second analysis. The fix needs a render id on stored
words, which lives in `internal/analysis` and the schema. Neither is owned here. The orchestrator
should carry this into the handoff log beside the analysis wiring task.

**The shifted words ignore the 10 ms crossfade at each cut.** A real defect, recorded as finding 2
at L. I measured it. I ran the real assembly graph from `planAssembly` through ffmpeg. The input
was a five second signal with a burst at 4.5 s, cut at 1 s and 3 s. The output puts the burst at
2.4800 s and lasts 2.9800 s. `renderClockWords` puts that moment at 2.5 s. A tap on a word after N joins
seeks N times 10 ms into it and clips its onset. A cold open adds up to two more fades. The
remediator's reason does not hold. The round 1 pin encoded the same 10 ms error. With the render's
fade, its "Two" starts at 0.99 s and the file lasts 1.99 s. The pin's intent, the render clock
rather than the raw clock, survives that correction. The fix should move that pin and
`render-clock.test.ts` by the fade. This path is the only one live today, since analysis never runs
yet.

**The gallery spec is not run by `npm run test:e2e`.** An acceptable limit, not a finding.
`web/tests/` and `web/package.json` sit outside `owns`, so the remediator could not gate it. The
editor's spec already follows the same pattern. The gated unit cases in `gallery-cards.test.ts`
cover `galleryCardKind`. Only the page's wiring from `kind` to the card rests on the ungated spec. I
ran it, and 4 passed. The orchestrator should scope a move into `web/tests/`.

## What the task delivers

- The detail sends edit words in seconds, the stem address, the newest render address and the
  rendered words. Every address is `/media/{id}`.
- The editor plays the stem and shows the stored words. Reverts post stored proposal ids, and stored
  decisions load the way the render reads them.
- The episode page plays the render on its own clock, keeps "playback waits" with no render, and
  links a draft to the editor.
- The gallery keeps a draft's progress card until its transcript pass is done, then opens the
  editor.

Both "Done when" clauses hold for a draft whose editorial pass has finished. Finding 1 covers the
window before that.
