## What surprised us

Reasoning tokens share the output cap with the answer. A scratch probe with the old settings on the same 10 minute fixture returned finish STOP with 3388 reasoning tokens and 302 answer tokens against the 4000 cap. That leaves almost no headroom. A real conversation needs more reasoning than looped sentences, so a 142 second take overflowed while a 32 second one fit. The fix bounds reasoning and doubles the cap, and the 30 minute ceiling now rests on clamped reasoning plus an answer that grows slowly with duration.

## Notes for the next developer

The reasoning budget is a ceiling, not a target, and the model spent all of it on the long take while still answering well. If answer quality ever slips on dense episodes, raise the budget before raising the cap, and keep the budget strictly below the cap. If a future model changes its reasoning range, the acceptance signal is the live probe, which will fail loudly on a rejected budget.
