## Notes for the remediator (not findings)

- Keyterms agree with `host.Load` on both checked seeds (the T2.2 golden order reproduces exactly),
  but they are not field-for-field identical: `NormalizeName` folds edge punctuation (`Lisbon.`
  joins `Lisbon`) where `host.keytermsFrom` splits on `strings.ToLower` only, and same-episode
  tie-breaks differ (row order versus episode number). The fold is arguably the better behaviour
  for a transcription boost. T4.2 wiring should pick one grouping deliberately.
- Normalisation has no false group found (folding touches only case, edge punctuation and spacing),
  but possessives, hyphen-versus-space and inner-period variants split (`Maya's` from `Maya`,
  `well-known` from `well known`, `St. Louis` from `St Louis`). The spec leaves the rule open, so
  this is a known limitation, not a defect.
- `CountMentions` zeros mean unknown, since any stored mention yields at least one. No consumer
  speaks counts yet. T4.2 must phrase absence as never-mentioned rather than a zero count.
- Nothing promises day spans. Counts speak episodes and mentions only, which matches episodes
  carrying no timestamps.
- Go style holds: named wire shapes, `ctx` first, wrapped sentinels, doc comments on exports, no
  `panic`, no `genai` import outside the provider seam, `gofmt` clean.
