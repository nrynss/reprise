server paths that reach `failed`.

  so the job stores it. The card reads `The transcript pass failed: <reason>.`

  episode (`cmd/reprise/main.go:2222`, `:2276`). `failInterruptedEpisodes` marks the episode failed.

  done with a title stored, and the card reads `The episode failed, and no pass stored a reason.`
  That is true, because no render job exists.
- **Editorial pass finished with nothing, or never started.** The card says so.

other states keep the early return they had at `931480d`. The note keys on the job id the card
follows, so two cards never share a note.
