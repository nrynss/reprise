## What it built

route gets its own gate budget inside a generous shared outer
budget, so one honest two-stem take passes with zero 429s while a
double-cadence burst trips the hammered route without blocking the
rest. Refusals still come from the gate, so retry hints agree.

a posted blob is unknown or foreign, and otherwise answers
`stems_not_found` with a `missing_media_ids` detail naming exactly
what is missing. The episode service already healed interrupted
linking, so the unrecoverable retry came from the handler pre-check
running before it. No contract change needed.
