## Why the intervals moved

User audio at 48 kHz, 16-bit, is 96000 bytes a second.
One 65536 byte chunk is about 0.68 seconds.
A 24 kHz host stem is about one chunk every 1.37 seconds.
Together that is about 2.2 chunk PUTs a second.
Two 48 kHz stems are about 2.9 a second.
A one minute refill stalls that take once the burst is gone.
The outer burst of 256 stalls it too, because `Mount` applies that budget to uploads.
Five a second clears 2.9 with margin.
I did not call the live edge and I did not open a paid session.
