## What surprised me

These routes set `Every` to one minute, so a cold client may spend the burst at once and then one request a minute.
The pin never advances the clock, so a passing pin does not measure a take spread over minutes.
A fresh upload bucket of 48 still cannot refuse chunk index 0.
