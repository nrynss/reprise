## What the commit does

The completion answer (`stemsComplete` in `cmd/reprise/main.go`) and episode
detail (`internal/api/episodes.go`) report the last transcript pass outcome
through a new `internal/episode/outcome.go` read. The schedule guard moves
from unfinished-only to latest-pass reconciliation (`episode.LastKindJob`),
so a repeat after a terminal silent pass reports the standing outcome and
starts nothing. The chain tail (`settleTranscript`) fails the episode on run
error or zero landed words. `CompleteStems` treats a repeat on a failed
episode as harmless, so the answer reports instead of refusing.
