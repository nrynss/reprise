## What it built

The editor screen draws a scripted draft with no backend. `fixture.ts`
holds timed words, three cuts with reasons, and proposals of all five
kinds addressed as word offsets into load order. The audio is a generated
tone of matching length wrapped in a blob URL.

`draft.ts` holds a framework-free `DraftController` over the library
model. `TranscriptEditor` carries words and cuts. `TranscriptFollower`
binds them to the player clock and seeks past cuts. `regionsFromCuts`
marks removed ranges on the waveform. `AudioPlayer` carries playback.
`computePeaksInWorker` buckets the peaks. `JobStream` follows a real
render job only when the backend starts one. Fixtures never open a
stream.

The screen shows proposed cuts as strikethrough with their reason and a
one-action revert each. Each revert writes a decision row locally first,
then posts it to the decisions endpoint. The cold open plays from its
range. Word clicks seek. The live word carries `aria-current` and a
visible mark. The canvas waveform shades removed ranges and seeks by
keyboard. Mark done arms, then confirms, then starts the render. That
confirm path is the only way out of the draft.

Every control is a native button or a labelled slider, and the page runs
both library gates on `?gate=1`.

## What surprised it

The runes-only tree scan rejects `$derived(` and `.get(` as text. The
screen now uses `$derived.by` and a small `queryValue` helper that
iterates the search entries. No store and no reactive statement entered
with either change.

The Chaaya `0.2.0` surface matched the plan exactly. `TranscriptEditor`,
`TranscriptFollower`, `regionsFromCuts`, `AudioPlayer`,
`computePeaksInWorker`, `JobStream`, `a11yGate`, and `contrastGate` all
carry the shapes the screen needs. No library gap arose, so no issue was
filed.

Playwright proofs cover the done criteria. Reverting a cut updates the
count and writes the decision row. A keyboard-only run reverts, previews
the cold open, and marks done. Word clicks move the readout. The gate
line reads passed. Unit pins cover the three cuts, the revert row, the
unknown revert, word seeking, cold open parking, the confirm step, the
query helper, and the backend fallback.

## Contract changes

The screen reads proposals of kinds `cold_open`, `cut`, `title`,
`show_notes`, and `callback`, with ranges as offsets into load order. It
posts `{ proposal_id, decision: "reverted" }` to
`/api/episodes/{id}/decisions` and posts to `/api/episodes/{id}/done` on
confirm. Both endpoints are stubs today. The screen works fully against
fixtures until they serve. The render change reads accepted cuts and the
cold open from the decision state this screen leaves behind.
