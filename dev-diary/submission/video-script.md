# Video script draft

Target shape: one played episode carries the video. Everything else gives context for that moment.

## Beat 1: cold open (0:00)

Play the strongest fifteen seconds of the demo episode. Show the episode title card while it plays. Let the audio sell the product before any narration starts.

[WAITS ON T6.4b] Pick the exact clip after the real voice sessions land. Choose delivery over content. Prefer laughter, a pause, or a thin voice.

## Beat 2: the problem (short)

Voiceover: every journaling tool records what you chose to say. None of them asks the follow-up. None of them asks again next week.

On screen: a notes app full of entries nobody reopened.

## Beat 3: the product in one line

Voiceover: Reprise is a personal podcast. You talk, a host asks, and the talk becomes an episode.

On screen: the live app at `https://reprise.nryn.dev`. The guest presses record.

## Beat 4: the callback

[WAITS ON T6.4b] Play the host opening with a reference to an earlier episode. Name the episode and the offset on screen. This beat is the thesis. Give it room.

Voiceover after the clip: it remembered. That is the whole product.

## Beat 5: how it works (fast, visual only)

Show four captions over app footage. Record. Review proposed cuts. Mark done. Take the episode to the gallery.

Voiceover: the model proposes every cut. You dispose of every cut. Nothing applies without your review.

## Beat 6: trust (short)

Voiceover: every episode stays private by default. Publishing takes one explicit action per episode. Sessions close explicitly, and the provider record confirms the close.

On screen: the private gallery badge. The closed session record with reason `client_end`.

## Beat 7: close

Voiceover: it asks the question you did not think to answer. Then it remembers the answer.

On screen: the title card, the live URL, and the repository link.

## Recording notes for the owner

Record narration after T6.4b picks the clips. Keep total length tight. Never describe a callback the episode does not contain.
