# Slide deck outline draft

Eight slides. Each judging criterion gets its own slide. The demo episode carries the presentation.

## 1. Title

Reprise. A personal podcast that remembers. Show the live URL and the repository link.

## 2. The problem

Journaling tools store what you chose to say. They never ask the follow-up. They never ask again next week.

## 3. The product

You talk, a host asks, and the talk becomes an episode. The episode ships with a cold open, chapters, and show notes.

## 4. Application of Technology

One WebSocket carries the live conversation. AssemblyAI runs transcription, turn taking, interruption, and voice end to end. The app records both sides locally as separate stems. The batch pass runs on the rendered file, so chapter timestamps describe the audio people hear.

Measured facts: scripted guests minted sessions with HTTP 201, streamed generated speech, and closed explicitly. Provider records confirm `client_end` closes. Connected calls cost about 2.1 cents for 16 seconds at 4.50 dollars per hour.

## 6. Business Value

Life story capture already earns subscription money. StoryWorth, Remento, and HereAfter prove families pay to preserve a voice. Reprise brings the same mechanism to a weekly show: an interviewer with memory that produces something listenable.

## 7. Originality

The show gets better at hearing you the longer you use it. Recurring names feed keyterm prompting. Early episodes mangle a name. Later episodes get it right because earlier ones taught them.

## 8. Trust and close

Episodes stay private by default. Anonymous media reads answer 404 with `Cache-Control: private, no-store`. Owner reads round trip byte exact. Close with the claim: it asks the question you did not think to answer, then remembers the answer.
